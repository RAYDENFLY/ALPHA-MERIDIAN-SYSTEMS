Market Screener Methodology & Normalization Standards

1. Turnover & OI Normalization: 24-hour volume is calculated strictly from quote turnover (USD/USDT), never raw base contract counts. Open Interest represents active contract notional value in USD.

2. Standardized Funding Rates: Rates across Hyperliquid (1h interval) and AsterDEX/Binance/Bybit (8h interval) are normalized to an 8-hour period and annualized (APR). Positive rates indicate Longs pay Shorts; negative rates indicate Shorts pay Longs.

3. Basis Spread / Price Divergence: Represents cross-venue mark price spread in basis points (bps) relative to the median benchmark price. This indicates order-book mid differences and basis variations, not guaranteed or executable arbitrage.

4. Research & Analysis Safety: This screener is strictly read-only and designed for manual market discovery. It contains zero trade recommendations, entry signals, stop losses, or automated execution triggers.
